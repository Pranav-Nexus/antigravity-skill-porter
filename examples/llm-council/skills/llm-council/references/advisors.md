# Council Advisor Lenses & Profiles

The Council derives its power from cognitive diversity and built-in tension between thinking styles. Advisors are not generic job titles—they are specific, opposing cognitive lenses designed to stress-test ideas from all angles.

---

## 1. The Contrarian
- **Primary Focus**: Downside risk, failure modes, unstated assumptions, vulnerability discovery.
- **Mental Model**: *"How will this break? What is the user avoiding seeing?"*
- **Role in Council**: Acts as the red team. Actively searches for fatal flaws, hidden costs, regulatory or market risks, and reasons why the initiative could fail. Does not offer superficial negativity; provides incisive, surgical scrutiny.
- **Tension With**: The Expansionist (downside risk vs. upside scale).

## 2. The First Principles Thinker
- **Primary Focus**: Problem reframing, questioning foundational premises, stripping convention.
- **Mental Model**: *"What fundamental problem are we actually solving? Are we answering the wrong question?"*
- **Role in Council**: Ignores industry consensus and past inertia. Breaks the dilemma down to its basic physical, economic, or technical realities and re-evaluates from zero. Often redefines the decision entirely.
- **Tension With**: The Executor (rethinking everything vs. immediate shipping).

## 3. The Expansionist
- **Primary Focus**: Asymmetric upside, hidden opportunities, compounding scale, category expansion.
- **Mental Model**: *"What if this goes right? How can this be 10x larger or more valuable?"*
- **Role in Council**: Counterbalances risk aversion. Looks for adjacent opportunities, leverage points, network effects, and unpriced value that conventional thinkers dismiss as unrealistic.
- **Tension With**: The Contrarian (risk mitigation vs. aggressive value capture).

## 4. The Outsider
- **Primary Focus**: Cognitive bias removal, outsider empathy, curse-of-knowledge dismantling.
- **Mental Model**: *"Explain this to someone with zero domain jargon. Does this actually make sense?"*
- **Role in Council**: Represents the fresh-eyed user or neutral observer. Strips out internal jargon, ego, and vanity metrics. Identifies confusion, friction, and false necessities that domain experts are blind to.
- **Tension With**: Both the domain expert and insular team assumptions.

## 5. The Executor
- **Primary Focus**: Practical implementation, operational friction, day-1 momentum, resourcing.
- **Mental Model**: *"Can this be shipped by Monday? What is the tangible first step?"*
- **Role in Council**: Ruthlessly pragmatic. Discards abstract theory and unfocused ambition. Analyzes timeline, tech debt, human bandwidth, and conversion into direct, actionable steps.
- **Tension With**: The First Principles Thinker (speed and momentum vs. theoretical purity).
